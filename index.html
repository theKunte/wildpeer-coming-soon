What it is

Your friends already own the gear.

Most outdoor gear spends the year in a closet. A tent for one weekend, snowshoes for one trip, skis a kid outgrows by spring. WildPeer lets a group of people who know each other share what they have: you list what you're happy to lend, borrow what you need, and the app remembers who has what and when it's due back.

How it works

Four steps, start to finish.

  1. 1

    Join a group you trust

    Start a group for your friends, family or club, or get invited to one. Nobody joins without an invitation.

  2. 2

    Share your gear

    Add photos and the condition of what you're willing to lend, and choose which of your groups can see it.

  3. 3

    Request and meet up

    Pick your dates. The owner says yes or no, and you agree where and when to hand it over.

  4. 4

    Bring it back

    Both of you confirm pickup and return, so there's a shared record and nothing goes missing.

Why it's different

Built on trust, not transactions.

  • No strangers

    There is no public marketplace. Only people in your groups can see your gear or ask to borrow it.

  • You stay in charge

    You approve every request, and you decide what a loan costs: nothing, or a coffee or a beer.

  • Private by default

    Other members see when something is due back, never who has it. Your email isn't shown to the group.

  • Made for groups

    Friend circles, ski carpools, families, outing clubs. The person who invites someone vouches for them.
